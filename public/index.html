Your data

What Orbit Axis stores, and why

Orbit Axis stores your email, your birth date, birth time, and birthplace, your saved charts, your active chart, your daily readings and their history, and your display preferences.

Birth details are needed to calculate your chart — the date places the planets, the time places the houses and angles, and the birthplace supplies the coordinates and timezone. Without a reliable time, Orbit Axis leaves houses and angles out rather than guessing at them.

Your information is private to your account. It is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers or other users.

Your chart, transits, moon phases, and daily readings are calculated by Orbit Axis's own astronomy engine. They do not depend on generative AI, and they keep working when AI is unavailable.

You can download everything at any time, and deleting your account removes all of it permanently.
