The twelve files in `openmoji-source/` are the unmodified upstream black SVGs
retrieved on 2026-08-20. Orbit's adapted versions remove the square emoji
container, retain the zodiac artwork from OpenMoji's `line-supplement` group,
crop the 72×72 source canvas to its 48×48 symbol field, and allow the stroke
color and display size to follow Orbit's templates. The adapted artwork in
`lib/orbit-x/celestial.js`, the generated standalone zodiac SVGs, and the
generated reference sheet are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
