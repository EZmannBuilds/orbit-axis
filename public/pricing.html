Pricing

Two plans. No countdowns, no fake scarcity, no pressure.

Orbit Axis presents astrology as symbolic reflection — a language for thinking about your own patterns. It is not prediction, and it is not medical, financial, legal, or relationship advice. Neither plan changes that.

Orbit Free — $0

Explore your chart and the sky. Free is the product, not a demo:

  • Your complete birth chart, calculated by Orbit's own astronomy engine
  • Today's sky and your daily reading, in plain language
  • The daily Tarot card and reflection
  • The Symbol Atlas
  • Your account, your data export, and account deletion — always free. Orbit never charges to give you your own data back.

Orbit Pro

Go deeper with more ways to compare, save, revisit and understand your symbolic patterns.

  • More saved charts — the people around you, not just yourself
  • Chart-to-chart compatibility readings
  • Expanded interpretations and technical sky detail
  • Your full reading history, unlimited
  • Chart photos and personalised reminders

What Pro costs

  • Monthly: $4.99 per month
  • Annual: $39 billed once per year. That is the full annual charge — about $3.25 a month, and $20.88 less than twelve months at the monthly price.

Prices are in USD. Cancel any time from Manage Billing; a cancelled subscription keeps Pro until the period you already paid for ends, and nothing renews after that.

How buying works

Payment runs through Stripe's hosted checkout — card details never touch Orbit's servers, and Orbit never sends Stripe your birth details, charts, readings, or anything beyond what payment needs. You must be signed in to your Orbit Axis account first, so the subscription lands on the right account. Manage or cancel any time from You → Manage billing.
